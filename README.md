# GDP-Per-Capita-and-Labor-Force-Participation-Rate-Analysis-With-R
The aim of this study is to compare the trajectories of Turkey’s real gross domestic product per capita and the labor force participation rate over 1990–2024, to make their relative changes visible on a common scale, and to discuss co‑movement and differences in growth pace between the two indicators. Because the series are measured in different units and direct level comparisons can be misleading, both are indexed to a common base year. In this setup, real GDP per capita defined by the World Bank’s WDI as output per person in constant prices is juxtaposed with the LFPR, which measures the share of the 15+ population that is either employed or actively seeking work.
